How to play

Ultimate Team: create a club, draft your core, then build the rest by opening packs, buying on the market, training cards and stacking chemistry. Play quick matches and solo challenges to earn coins and packs. Franchise is the classic season mode. The Animation lab previews every animation and replay, and Import data loads your own teams, players and stats.

Pick a team, play 17 weeks against a real game clock, and chase the Grand Bowl. You call plays and run the offense. Opponent drives are simulated play by play. Gameplay is keyboard only; the mouse just clicks through menus and draws routes.

Playbook: type to search, Tab changes category, arrow keys choose, Enter calls the play. Before the snap, press R to draw a new route for any receiver and S to save it to your Custom plays.

Passing: WASD moves the QB. Click and drag on the field to aim, then release to throw to that spot. Hold Shift for a lob. Lead your receiver, and watch out: defenders read where you are aiming and will jump routes. Press Space right as he is about to catch it to hit the green.

Running: W and S steer, D bursts, A slows. J jukes, K spins, L hurdles, H stiff arms. Time them as a defender closes in. Mistime one and you will pay for it.

Every touchdown you score gets a 3D cinematic replay.

Import data

Load your own teams, players and statistics as JSON. You can paste it, load a .json file, or try a URL. Published claude.ai pages block requests to other sites, so for an API, save its response as a file first.

Format

An object with teams and players arrays (and optionally stats). Field names are matched loosely:

Teams: id, city, name, abbr or abbreviation, color and alternateColor (hex). Players: name (or firstName and lastName), position, team (id, abbreviation or name), number, headshot (an image link, shown on the touchdown card), celebration (a signature touchdown celebration: dunk, dj, bowling, fly, crown or knight; dunk only happens for players given it), and either overall, ratings (speed, strength, agility, catching, routeRunning, throwPower, throwAccuracy, awareness, passBlock, runBlock, passRush, tackle, coverage, kickPower, kickAccuracy) or stats (passingYards, rushingYards, receivingYards, touchdowns, sacks, tackles, interceptions and so on). Missing ratings are filled in from overall, and a missing overall is estimated from stats, ranked within each position.

Paste overalls

Have a ratings list, like Madden overalls? Paste one player per line as Name, 91 (tabs or spaces work too) and they become each player's baseline. Teams, packs and seasons all use them.

Player baselines

Each player's baseline overall drives all of his card versions. Origins sit about 10 below, program cards above, and every card's ceiling is baseline plus 10. If your data has no overall, baselines are estimated from stats ranked within each position. Change any number here to set it yourself, for example to match Madden. Ratings shift with it. Cards you already own keep their current numbers; new pulls use the new baseline.
